# Calculation scope and rates

Verified against the sources below on 1 October 2026. Ledgr estimates annual liabilities and the effect of deductions; it does not calculate a tax refund from PAYG withholding or lodge a return.

## Supported estimate

- Full-year resident, non-resident and ordinary working-holiday income-tax brackets. Working-holiday residents eligible for treaty rates can select that exception in Settings.
- Resident low-income tax offset and the ordinary single-adult Medicare low-income reduction.
- Single-person Medicare levy surcharge with full-year hospital-cover/exemption choices. Reportable fringe benefits affect both the surcharge base and threshold; reportable super contributions and net investment losses affect the threshold.
- HELP repayments, including marginal rates from 2025–26, the 10% ceiling, an optional remaining-debt cap and repayment-income additions. Recorded net rental losses are added back automatically; other reportable amounts are entered in Settings.
- Eligible employee deductions, depreciation, fixed-rate/actual-cost WFH, car claims capped at 5,000 work kilometres per car per financial year, and ownership-adjusted rental income/expenses.
- FIFO matching of entered crypto/share transactions. Asset kind and symbol identify separate holdings. Discount eligibility uses the calendar anniversary and excludes acquisition/disposal dates; quantities retain crypto precision.

Family/dependant thresholds, senior offsets, part-year residency or insurance, foreign-income offsets and specialised CGT residency/exemption rules are not modelled. The CGT worksheet assumes the ordinary individual 50% discount and that entered gains are assessable; foreign or changing residency needs separate assessment. Acquisition history must be complete for cost bases and carried losses to be reliable.

## Versioned amounts

| Financial year | Car rate | HELP marginal thresholds | MLS single thresholds | Medicare single low-income threshold |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: |
| 2024–25 | 88c/km | Prior flat-band system | $97,000 / $113,000 / $151,000 | $27,222 |
| 2025–26 | 88c/km | $67,000 / $125,000 | $101,000 / $118,000 / $158,000 | $28,011 |
| 2026–27 | 91c/km | $69,528 / $129,717 | $105,000 / $123,000 / $164,000 | $28,011 provisionally |

The 2026–27 Medicare threshold uses the current legislated amount until the next annual amendment. WFH is 70c/hour in the supported years. Bracket comparisons are continuous for cents, with the resulting estimated liability rounded to cents; this is not a replication of every ATO assessment rounding rule.
